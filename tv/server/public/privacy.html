Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FREEVEE ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our web application and media streaming services.

1. Information We Collect

When you authenticate with FREEVEE using your Google Account, we receive access to specific basic profile and authorization data:

  • Google Profile Data: Your name, email address, and profile picture provided via Google OAuth 2.0.
  • Google Drive Read-Only Access: Metadata (file names, IDs, sizes, and folder hierarchy) for videos in folders you explicitly select.
  • Application State: Your Continue Watching playback timestamps, favorites, and folder preferences stored locally on your instance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to provide core media streaming functionality:

  • To authenticate your session and isolate your personal library from other users.
  • To scan and index media files contained inside your chosen Google Drive folders.
  • To stream requested video files directly from Google Drive to your authenticated browser or device.
  • To maintain your personal playback progress and favorites list.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

FREEVEE’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We do not sell your Google user data or Google Drive contents to third parties.
  • We do not use your Google Drive files or personal data for advertising or marketing.
  • We do not use your data to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Human access to your private Google Drive files is strictly prohibited.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your OAuth tokens and preferences are stored securely on the private server hosting your FREEVEE instance. We do not store copies of your video files on our server hard drives; all video playback is streamed directly on-demand via secure HTTPS byte-range requests.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can disconnect your Google Account at any time by signing out or revoking FREEVEE's access in your Google Account Security Settings. Upon revocation, all cached tokens and session access are immediately terminated.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please reach out to the administrator of this FREEVEE instance.
