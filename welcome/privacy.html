Privacy Policy

Application: butfree (https://butfree.online) • Last Updated: October 4, 2026 • Effective Date: October 4, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to butfree ("we", "our", "us", or "the Service"), accessible at https://butfree.online. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

butfree provides an umbrella entertainment platform and Bring-Your-Own-Storage (BYOS) cloud client that connects to your personal Google Drive to index, organize, read, stream, and play your comic books, movies, TV episodes, music albums, audiobooks, and video game ROMs across dedicated web, mobile, and TV applications.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we safeguard it, and your rights concerning your data and connected Google services.

2. Google API Data & Limited Use Disclosure

butfree uses Google APIs (specifically the Google Drive API and Google Identity Services) to allow you to authenticate and access media files stored in your own Google Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance Statement

butfree's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically regarding your Google data:

  • No External Storage of Media Files: butfree does not copy, upload, or transfer the contents of your Google Drive files to our own servers or databases. Media files (video, audio, comic archives, game files) are streamed directly between your personal Google Drive storage and your client device (web browser, Android app, or TV client).
  • No Selling of User Data: We never sell, rent, lease, trade, or monetize your Google user data or file metadata to any third party.
  • No Advertising or Tracking: Your Google user data is never used to serve targeted advertisements or build behavioral profiles.
  • No AI Model Training: We never use your Google Drive contents, file metadata, or personal library information to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Human Review Restrictions: No human at butfree reads or inspects your personal files unless you explicitly request customer support assistance and provide explicit consent, or where required for security investigation or legal compliance.

3. Information We Collect and Receive

We collect and process the following categories of information strictly to provide the core services of butfree:

A. Information Received via Google OAuth

  • Account Identity: Your Google display name, email address, and profile avatar URL used exclusively for account creation, login authentication, and display within the app header.
  • OAuth Access and Refresh Tokens: Securely stored authorization tokens allowing the application to query Google Drive APIs on your behalf.

B. Google Drive Information Accessed

  • File Metadata: File IDs, filenames, folder hierarchies, mime types, and file sizes for supported media formats (such as .cbz, .cbr, .pdf, .epub, .mp4, .mkv, .flac, .mp3, .m4b, .nes, .gba).
  • Media Stream Data: Byte-range streams initiated directly by your client device to read comic pages, play audio tracks, or stream video feeds.

C. Application State and Preferences Data

  • Reading bookmarks (e.g. current page in a comic book or chapter in an audiobook).
  • Video playback progress timestamps and watched status.
  • Retro game save state files (stored in your personal Google Drive application folder).
  • Client theme and UI preferences.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • Authenticating your session and confirming your identity.
  • Generating your personal library index and fetching public metadata (posters, issue summaries, artist information) matching your filenames.
  • Directly serving media streams and comic pages to your connected devices.
  • Maintaining cross-device synchronization of reading progress, playback positions, and save states.
  • Providing technical support and troubleshooting bugs when requested by you.

5. Data Storage, Security, and Encryption

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

  • All communication between your browser/apps, butfree infrastructure, and Google Drive endpoints is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest using strong AES-256 encryption.
  • Media files are never persistently stored on our servers; they stream ephemerally straight from Google's content delivery servers to your client device.

6. Data Retention, Account Deletion & Revoking Permissions

You have complete sovereignty over your data and connections:

  • Disconnecting Google Drive: You can disconnect your Google Drive connection at any time from within the application settings by clicking "Disconnect Drive". This immediately purges local cached tokens and index data.
  • Revoking Access via Google: You can permanently revoke butfree's access to your Google account at any time through the official Google Account Security / Third-Party Apps with Account Access dashboard.
  • Complete Account Deletion: If you wish to delete your butfree account and all associated synchronized preference data, you may submit a request to support@butfree.online. We will process your deletion within 30 days.

7. Third-Party Services and External Links

Our website and applications interact with:

  • Google APIs: Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Public Metadata APIs: For example, open metadata providers for cover art, posters, and track listings. We send only non-identifying query titles (such as movie names or comic titles) to retrieve cover artwork.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When modifications occur, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
