Privacy & Architecture

How butfree Works with Google Drive

We believe your data belongs to you. butfree operates on a pure BYOS (Bring Your Own Storage) model where your Google Drive remains the sole owner and host of your files.

Direct Client Streaming

Media files stream directly from Google's servers to your web browser or client device. butfree servers never proxy, store, or hold copies of your files.

Scoped & Transparent Scopes

We request only the Google Drive permissions required to read and index the media directories you select. You retain complete control to revoke access anytime.

Zero Data Selling or Ads

Your library metadata and personal files are never sold, rented, or utilized for advertising or training artificial intelligence models.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

butfree's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
